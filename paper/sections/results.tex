\subsection{E1: baseline reproduction and the extrapolation problem}
On OGW (100~kHz, OBS+BSS over a baseline pool at $\leq 40\,^\circ$C),
the compensated residual energy of \emph{healthy} records rises from
$0.043 \pm 0.014$ within the baseline range to $0.303 \pm 0.623$ in the
extrapolated $40$--$60\,^\circ$C range---a $7.0	imes$ inflation that
drives false alarms (Fig.~
ef{fig:extrap} [TBD]). This reproduces, on
real data, the known fragility of compensation outside the baseline
temperature range and motivates compensation-free discrimination (C2).

\subsection{E2: eventization Pareto and the structure advantage}
At high data rates both SoD events and matched-rate uniform decimation
retain detection (AUC $=1.0$ on synthetic OGW-like data). The difference
emerges in tasks that need the damage wavepacket's \emph{time
structure}. Coarse localization from event-cluster arrival times stays
accurate as the event rate drops (error $48.5	o31.2$~mm from rate
$0.75	o 10^{-3}$), whereas uniform decimation at the same rate
\emph{degrades} ($44.6	o53.5$~mm)---event sampling preferentially
keeps the informative scattering transient that decimation averages
away (Fig.~
ef{fig:pareto} [TBD]).

\subsection{E3: temperature--damage discrimination}
Using only event-stream structure (cross-path coherence, localization,
event count, arrival-time spread) at 40~kHz, a logistic discriminator
separates healthy-temperature-drift records from damage records with
cross-validated AUC $0.814$; the event count alone gives $0.846$ and
coherence $0.789$. Temperature produces high-coherence events (many
paths active together), damage a localized subset---confirming the
structural prior on real data without any learned temperature model.

\subsection{E4: long-term level-B validation}
On the 2021-04 outdoor month (reversible-mass onset at record 8401 of
15069), level-A event counts are eventized per path and a
path-isolation score (max-path minus median-path event activity)
separates damage from temperature: the damage segment scores
$3.5	imes$ the healthy one. At a false-alarm rate of $pprox 0.1\%$
(8 false events over 8401 healthy records) the mass is detected with a
latency of $5509$ records ($pprox 269$~h). The reversible mass is
deliberately subtle; permanent damage (dent, through-hole) produces a
larger persistent step. Confounded sensor-drift segments are reported
separately, not merged into these metrics.

\subsection{E5: directional analysis}
Binning the 66 paths by propagation direction, the damage-induced
event activity is concentrated at $15^\circ$ and $105^\circ$
(directional coefficient of variation $0.69$, versus $0$ for an
isotropic response)---a measurable directional dependence even in the
quasi-isotropic layup, consistent with layup-detail anisotropy. We
state this conservatively as direction-resolved event structure rather
than strong anisotropy validation.
