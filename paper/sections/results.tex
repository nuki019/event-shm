\subsection{E1: baseline reproduction and the extrapolation problem}
On OGW (100~kHz, OBS+BSS over a baseline pool at $\leq 40\,^\circ$C), the compensated residual energy of \emph{healthy} records rises from $0.043 \pm 0.014$ within the baseline range to $0.303 \pm 0.623$ in the extrapolated $40$--$60\,^\circ$C range---a $7.0\times$ inflation that drives false alarms (Fig.~
ef{fig:extrap}). This reproduces, on real data, the known fragility of compensation outside the baseline temperature range and motivates compensation-free discrimination (C2).

\subsection{E2: detection Pareto at the sensitive frequency}
At the damage-sensitive frequency (40~kHz, Section~E6), the full-resolution baseline reaches per-record AUC $0.953$ (D04) and $0.851$ (D24). SoD eventization preserves this detection at extreme compression: at rate $2\times10^{-4}$ events/sample ($\approx 5000\times$) D04 is detected at AUC $1.000$, and at $10^{-3}$ D24 at $0.928$---in both cases \emph{above} matched-rate uniform decimation ($0.991$ and $0.804$), with a clear optimum at intermediate threshold $\delta$ (Fig.~
ef{fig:pareto}). This is the headline result: acquisition-side eventization retains detection at three orders of magnitude below the raw rate.

\subsection{E2b: eventization preserves the scattering transient}
At matched data rate we compare SoD eventization against uniform decimation on \emph{how well the damage-scattering envelope is preserved} (correlation against the full-resolution envelope in the high-energy window). On real OGW records (100~kHz, both D04 and D24), the two are equivalent at high rate, but below $\sim 10^{-2}$ events/sample the SoD reconstruction keeps fidelity $\approx 0.6$ while uniform decimation collapses to $\approx 0.06$---a $10\times$ advantage (Fig.~
ef{fig:fidelity}). On synthetic OGW-like data the same advantage appears in coarse localization (error $31$~mm event vs $54$~mm uniform at rate $10^{-3}$). Detection of the weak surface-disc damage remains hard at these rates (AUC $\approx 0.6$), which is precisely the regime where the level-B structure channel (below) is needed.

\subsection{E3: temperature--damage discrimination}
Using only event-stream structure (cross-path coherence, localization, event count, arrival-time spread) at 40~kHz, a logistic discriminator separates healthy-temperature-drift records from damage records with cross-validated AUC $0.814$; the event count alone gives $0.846$ and coherence $0.789$. Temperature produces high-coherence events (many paths active together), damage a localized subset---confirming the structural prior on real data without any learned temperature model.

\subsection{E4: long-term level-B validation}
On the 2021-04 outdoor month (reversible-mass onset at record 8401 of 15069), level-A event counts are eventized per path and a path-isolation score (max-path minus median-path event activity) separates damage from temperature: the damage segment scores $3.5\times$ the healthy one. At a false-alarm rate of $\approx 0.1\%$ (8 false events over 8401 healthy records) the mass is detected with a latency of $5509$ records ($\approx 269$~h). The reversible mass is deliberately subtle; permanent damage (dent, through-hole) produces a larger persistent step. Confounded sensor-drift segments are reported separately, not merged into these metrics.

\subsection{E5: directional analysis}
Binning the 66 paths by propagation direction, the damage-induced event activity is concentrated at $15^\circ$ and $105^\circ$ (directional coefficient of variation $0.69$, versus $0$ for an isotropic response)---a measurable directional dependence even in the quasi-isotropic layup, consistent with layup-detail anisotropy. We state this conservatively as direction-resolved event structure rather than strong anisotropy validation.

\subsection{E6: frequency selection and ablation}
Detection of the weak surface discs is strongly frequency-dependent (Fig.~
ef{fig:freq}): both D04 and D24 are detected near-perfectly at 40~kHz (AUC $1.000$ and $0.953$) but fall toward chance by $\geq 120$~kHz (AUC $\approx 0.44$--$0.48$). Compensation quality dominates the detection chain (Table~
ef{tab:ablation}): at 40~kHz, no compensation gives AUC $0.583$, OBS alone $0.843$, and OBS+BSS $1.000$.
