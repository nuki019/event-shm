\subsection{E1: baseline reproduction and the extrapolation problem}
On OGW (100~kHz, OBS+BSS over a baseline pool at $\leq 40\,^\circ$C),
the compensated residual energy of \emph{healthy} records rises from
$0.043 \pm 0.014$ within the baseline range to $0.303 \pm 0.623$ in the
extrapolated $40$--$60\,^\circ$C range---a $7.0	imes$ inflation that
drives false alarms (Fig.~
ef{fig:extrap} [TBD]). This reproduces, on
real data, the known fragility of compensation outside the baseline
temperature range and motivates compensation-free discrimination (C2).

\subsection{E2: eventization Pareto and the structure advantage}
At high data rates both SoD events and matched-rate uniform decimation
retain detection (AUC $=1.0$ on synthetic OGW-like data). The difference
emerges in tasks that need the damage wavepacket's \emph{time
structure}. Coarse localization from event-cluster arrival times stays
accurate as the event rate drops (error $48.5	o31.2$~mm from rate
$0.75	o 10^{-3}$), whereas uniform decimation at the same rate
\emph{degrades} ($44.6	o53.5$~mm)---event sampling preferentially
keeps the informative scattering transient that decimation averages
away (Fig.~
ef{fig:pareto} [TBD]).
