\subsection{E7: bounded SoD does not lead the hard-cap comparison}
All observed packets satisfy their declared record capacity. The
training-fitted signal quantizer clips no samples in training, validation,
D04, or D24 and clips two of 70,075,368 healthy test samples. Temperature
matching yields 81 unique pairs for each condition; the largest matched
temperature difference is 0.075~$^\circ$C for D04 and 0.108~$^\circ$C for
D24, both below the 0.25~$^\circ$C protocol limit. Across capacities,
bounded SoD has damage-higher paired win rates of 0.556--0.617 for D04 and
0.568--0.605 for D24, whereas every general-codec/condition combination is
0.963--0.975. Thus, the ordering also holds in the predeclared
temperature-matched diagnostic; this remains a within-plate comparison, not
an independent structural replication.

Table~\ref{tab:codec} reports every predeclared capacity. Bounded SoD has
D04 AUC 0.535--0.552 and D24 AUC 0.546--0.581. Uniform linear interpolation,
PCA, and Haar DWT have higher point AUC at every capacity for both conditions.
This is a negative result for SoD under the declared comparison; it is not a
claim that any tested codec is universally optimal. PCA model bytes are
listed separately because the fitted decoder is substantial even though it is
not amortized into a per-record packet.

\subsection{E8: neither alarm feature yields an operational result}
The April replay has 8,401 labelled healthy-prefix records, corresponding to
16.72 days of healthy exposure. Across all threshold settings, no
pre-onset incident remains active at the labelled onset, so the reported
delay is always a newly started post-onset incident. Table~\ref{tab:alarm}
shows the complete grid range for each feature. Every threshold was derived
from March only; a range in the table is a range over nine frozen operating
points, not an uncertainty interval or a chosen working point.

Dense residual energy produces 0.897--5.144 false calls/day, and Level-A SoD
event count produces 0.897--4.964 false calls/day. Their first newly started
post-onset alarms occur 2,556.2--2,584.8 minutes after onset, while record
exceedance coverage remains at most 25.6\%. These observations do not support
an operational alarm claim. They also do not estimate a population
probability of detection because April contributes one observed labelled
transition.
