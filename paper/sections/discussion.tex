\paragraph{A substantive negative codec result.}
The strict comparison yields a clear decision within its scope: bounded SoD
is not the preferred codec among the four tested implementations. This is not
an absence of a conclusion. The conclusion follows because the same
date-separated protocol, hard-cap accounting, record-level metric, and
all-capacity reporting are applied to each codec, yet SoD is lower in held-out
AUC at every capacity for both D04 and D24. It would be incorrect to restore
the historical matched-count comparison as a headline because sample counts
do not charge SoD timestamp framing or establish parity with general codecs.

\paragraph{What the alarm audit says.}
The cold-start replay is also unfavorable. Across both frozen feature grids,
false calls remain nonzero on the future healthy prefix, the first new
post-onset alarm is delayed by roughly 42.6--43.1 hours, and post-onset
record exceedance is sparse. Reporting this joint outcome is more informative
than reporting one threshold with the smallest false-call rate or the largest
coverage. It does not establish a calibrated field false-alarm rate, and it
does not estimate probability of detection from a single transition.

\paragraph{Evaluation boundaries.}
The result is tied to a post-compensation software representation, one CFRP
plate with reversible disc conditions, two damage labels, and the implemented
codec families. The April replay has a 5.5\% temperature-support gap relative
to March and one labelled onset. None of those data measure acquisition-side
sampling, embedded memory, energy, real-time latency, sensor ageing, packet
loss, or industrial interference. The reported PCA model sizes also caution
against interpreting a per-record payload alone as a full deployment cost.

\paragraph{Next evidence.}
The next defensible step is a preregistered multi-structure study with
independent calibration and future test periods, the same hard-cap accounting,
and predeclared alert criteria. A hardware claim would additionally require
measured firmware memory, throughput, energy, and end-to-end latency. Those
experiments could change the scope of the conclusion, but they cannot be
inferred from the present replay.
