\subsection{Two-level send-on-delta eventization}
Let $x_p^{(k)}(t)$ be the guided-wave record of path $p$ at monitoring
epoch $k$, sampled at $f_s$. After temperature compensation against a
baseline $\hat{x}_p^{(k)}$ (Section~\ref{sec:comp}), we form the
residual $r_p^{(k)}(t) = x_p^{(k)}(t) - \hat{x}_p^{(k)}(t)$.

\paragraph{Level A: within-record events.}
SoD sampling of $r$ with threshold $\delta$: an event
$e = (t_i, s_i)$ is emitted at sample $t_i$ whenever
$r(t_i)$ crosses the last emitted level by $\pm\delta$, with sign
$s_i = \mathrm{sgn}(\Delta r)$. A perfect compensation of a healthy
record yields $r \approx 0$ and hence almost no events; a scattering
wavepacket from damage produces a time-localized cluster of events
whose arrival time encodes the damage--path geometry
(Fig.~\ref{fig:method} [TBD]).

\paragraph{Level B: across-record events.}
Each record is summarized by a damage index
$\mathrm{DI}_p(k)$ (Section~\ref{sec:di}). SoD eventization of the
slowly varying series $\mathrm{DI}_p(k)$ with threshold $\delta_B$
emits an event only when the DI changes materially. A 4.5-year stream
of $\sim 10^6$ records per path compresses to $10^2$--$10^4$ events, a
$10^3$--$10^4\times$ reduction.

\subsection{Temperature compensation (baseline)}
\label{sec:comp}
We use OBS+BSS \cite{croxford2010}: select the baseline minimizing
residual energy from a pool (OBS), then stretch it in time by a factor
$\alpha \in [1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon]$ minimizing residual energy (BSS).
Importantly, level-B eventization does \emph{not} depend on
within-record compensation quality, providing an independent detection
channel.

\subsection{Damage indices}
\label{sec:di}
We use the normalized residual energy
$\mathrm{DI}_p(k) = \|r_p^{(k)}\|_2^2 \,/\, \|x_p^{(k)}\|_2^2$
and, for eventized records, the energy of the zero-order-hold
reconstruction of the residual from its SoD events.

\subsection{Temperature--damage discrimination from event structure}
\label{sec:disc}
Two scalar statistics of the per-record event counts
$\{n_p\}_{p=1}^{P}$ across the $P$ paths capture the physics:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Cross-path coherence}
  $c = \frac{1}{P}\sum_p \mathbb{1}[n_p \geq \theta]$:
  fraction of paths simultaneously active. Temperature acts globally
  ($c \to 1$); damage is localized ($c \ll 1$).
\item \emph{Path-localization index}
  $\ell = \sum_{p \in \text{top-}10\%} n_p / \sum_p n_p$:
  concentration of the event mass on a few paths.
\end{itemize}
A rule-based discriminator scores a record as temperature-like when
$c$ is high and damage-like when $\ell$ is high; a logistic regression
on $(c, \ell, \bar{n}, \sigma_n)$ provides the learned variant.
At level B, damage shows a sudden, persistent rise of $\mathrm{DI}_p$
events on a path subset, whereas temperature produces slow,
seasonally-correlated events on all paths.
