This work replaces exploratory sample-count comparisons with a strict
post-compensation codec benchmark and a cross-month cold-start alarm replay.
All configurations are selected without test labels, all record packets are
charged by serialized bytes, and all predeclared capacities and threshold
grids are reported. On the held-out OGW conditions, bounded SoD does not
outperform uniform linear interpolation, PCA, or Haar DWT at any tested
capacity. On the March-to-April replay, neither tested alarm feature provides
an operational result under the reported false-call, newly started delay, and
coverage outcomes.

These are scope-bounded negative findings, not evidence about general
deployment, hardware cost, or population probability of detection. Future
work requires independent structures and transitions together with measured
endpoint performance under a preregistered protocol.
