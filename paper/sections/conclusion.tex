This work replaces exploratory sample-count comparisons with a strict
post-compensation codec benchmark and a cross-month cold-start alarm replay.
All configurations are selected without test labels, all record packets are
charged by serialized bytes, and all predeclared capacities and threshold
grids are reported. The resulting applicability boundary has three parts:
bounded SoD does not lead held-out record AUC against uniform linear
interpolation, PCA, or Haar DWT on the OGW conditions at any tested capacity
under the declared per-record capacity contract; the March-to-April replay
cannot establish an operational-alarm claim; and the two results are not a
shared mechanism study or a deployment validation.

These are scope-bounded negative findings, not evidence about general SoD
behavior, external confirmation, hardware cost, field false-alarm calibration,
or population probability of detection. Future work requires independently
eligible structures and transitions under a preregistered protocol, together
with measured endpoint performance before any hardware claim is made.
